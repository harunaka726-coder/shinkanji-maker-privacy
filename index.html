English

This Privacy Policy explains how Original Kanji Maker (“the App”) handles information when you use the mobile application. The App lets you create new kanji characters by combining radicals and parts, keep a local notebook of saved kanji (including readings and meanings you edit), adjust language settings, and optionally export or import your data. No account is required.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data stored on your device

The App stores the following information locally on your device (for example via AsyncStorage or similar on-device storage):

  • Kanji you create or save in your notebook
  • Readings, meanings, and other notes you enter
  • Language and other in-app preferences
  • Data you choose to export or import (handled on your device unless you share the file yourself)

This data remains on your device. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not receive a copy of your notebook contents as part of normal App use.

1.2 Advertising and analytics-related data

The App may display advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) or related Google advertising services. Advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Device identifiers (for example Advertising ID / AAID / IDFA, where available and permitted)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information
  • Device type, operating system, and app/version information
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic information

This information is used to serve, measure, and improve ads, detect fraud, and comply with applicable advertising policies. Google’s processing of such data is governed by Google’s own policies. See: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Help.

1.3 Information we do not collect by default

  • We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or password to use the App.
  • We do not intentionally collect precise GPS location for App features.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. How Information Is Used

  • On-device features: to provide kanji creation, notebook storage, language settings, and optional export/import.
  • Advertising: to show ads and measure ad performance via AdMob / Google Mobile Ads.
  • Support: if you contact us through the forms below, we use the information you submit only to respond to your inquiry.

3. Data Sharing

Notebook and preference data stay on your device unless you export a file and share it yourself. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in their policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention

Local App data remains on your device until you delete it within the App, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Advertising partners retain data according to their own retention policies. Support messages you send via contact forms are retained only as needed to handle your request.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App or a contact form, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Your Choices

  • You can delete notebook entries or clear App data on your device at any time.
  • You can limit ad personalization through your device settings (for example, reset or limit the Advertising ID on Android / App Tracking Transparency and related settings on iOS, where available).
  • You may decline optional permissions the App or ad SDK requests; some features or ads may be limited as a result.

7. International Processing

Advertising and contact-form providers may process data on servers outside your country. By using the App or contacting us, you acknowledge that such processing may occur subject to the providers’ terms and applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

これらのデータは原則として端末内に留まります。本アプリはユーザーアカウントを 運用せず、通常の利用においてノート内容のコピーを運営者が受け取ることはありません。

  • 端末の種類、OS、アプリ／バージョン情報
  • 広告の表示・クリック等のインタラクション情報および診断情報

これらの情報は、広告の配信・効果測定・改善、不正防止、広告ポリシーの遵守などに 用いられます。Google による取扱いは Google のポリシーに従います。詳細は Google プライバシーポリシー および AdMob ヘルプ をご参照ください。

  • 個人情報を販売することはありません。
